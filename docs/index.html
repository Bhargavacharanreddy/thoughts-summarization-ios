Mull

Capture every thought. Let AI make sense of it.

Mull is an iPhone app for brain dumps. Speak or type whatever is on your mind, and AI sorts your thoughts into categories, builds a prioritized task list, and writes a daily summary with an illustration. By default it all runs privately on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Frequently asked questions

How do I record a thought with the Action Button?

Open the iPhone Settings → Action Button, swipe to Shortcut, tap Choose a Shortcut, find Mull and pick Record a Thought. Press the button to start recording; press again or pause speaking to save. You can also say “Record a thought in Mull” to Siri.

Do I need an API key?

No. With Apple Intelligence (the default), every feature works on your iPhone for free. OpenAI and Claude are optional; they need your own API key, and the app shows step-by-step how to get one.

Why isn't a summary image appearing?

With Apple Intelligence, images are made by Image Playground. Make sure Apple Intelligence is turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri and the Image Playground app is installed. Claude can't create images — switch to Apple Intelligence or OpenAI.

How do I delete my data?

In Mull, open Settings → Data to clear summaries and history, tasks, or everything. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Is my data private?

Yes. See the Privacy Policy.
