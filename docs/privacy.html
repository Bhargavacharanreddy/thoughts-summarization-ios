Privacy Policy

Mull — AI Brain Dump · Effective November 1, 2026

The short version

  • Mull has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The developer runs no servers and never receives your data.
  • Your thoughts, summaries, images and tasks are stored only on your iPhone.
  • By default, all AI runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
  • Your data goes to OpenAI or Anthropic only if you choose that provider, add your own API key and agree in the app.

Data stored on your device

Mull stores the thoughts you type or speak, the categories, daily summaries, summary images and tasks it creates, and your settings. This data stays on your iPhone in the app's private storage. It is removed when you delete the app, or anytime from Settings → Data in the app.

If you add an OpenAI or Anthropic API key, it is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is only ever sent to that provider to authenticate your requests.

Microphone and speech recognition

Mull uses the microphone only while you are recording a thought. Speech is converted to text with Apple's Speech framework, on your iPhone whenever your language supports on-device recognition. Where it doesn't, Apple may process the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. A temporary audio file is kept only until the thought is saved and is then deleted.

AI providers

Apple Intelligence (default)

Categorizing, summarizing, task planning, transcript cleanup and image creation (Image Playground) run on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties.

OpenAI (optional)

If you select OpenAI and agree in the app, Mull sends your thought text, voice recordings (for transcription) and summaries directly from your iPhone to OpenAI using your own API key. OpenAI handles that data under the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Anthropic Claude (optional)

If you select Claude and agree in the app, Mull sends your thought text and transcripts directly from your iPhone to Anthropic using your own API key. Anthropic handles that data under the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

You can switch back to Apple Intelligence at any time, and you can remove your API key from Settings.

Children

Mull is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
